# Economy Control

[Українською](README.uk.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md)

**Version 1.0.0.0 · Farming Simulator 25 · PC/Mac**

Your farm. Your country. Your economic history.

The economy never stands still. Crop, fuel, fertilizer, machinery and land prices change. What is profitable today may be unprofitable tomorrow.

Economy Control adds a dynamic economy to Farming Simulator 25, based on historical economic data and adapted to the game's mechanics.

Develop your farm under changing market conditions, plan investments and adapt to new economic circumstances.

## 12 countries to choose from

- Hungary

- Poland

Each country has its own economic indicators, historical market trends and exchange rates.

## Economic history from 2015 to 2026

Choose a starting year and develop your farm under the economic conditions of that period.

Historical data is used to model changes across markets. Calculated estimates are used for periods and sectors with incomplete statistical data.

## More than simply increasing difficulty

Economy Control does not apply a single difficulty multiplier to all goods.

Economic sectors develop differently. Fuel costs may rise while crop prices fall. Supported machinery, farmland, resources and services have their own economic trends.

Game prices are adapted to historical economic trends rather than reproducing real-world market prices on a 1:1 basis.

## The economy does not stop in 2026

After the historical period, the economy continues to develop through an internal simulation.

Markets interact, economic conditions change and farm development calls for new decisions. The future economy is a gameplay simulation, not a forecast of real economic events.

The mod uses its own economic calendar without changing the physical game calendar, weather, map or field conditions.

## Compatibility and special integrations

Economy Control works independently and does not require other mods.

Dedicated integration mechanisms are provided for:

- Admin Shop Prices
- Additional Currencies
- Production Revamp
- WorkerCosts
- Red Tape
- Field Leasing
- Rent Your Field
- Production Point Leasing
- Bank Credit
- Hire Purchasing
- Net Worth Tracker
- Better Contracts
- RDM Dynamic Dealership
- Advanced Damage System

These integrations cover supported pricing operations, exchange rates, production, leasing, wages, tax accounting and asset valuation. The extent of integration depends on third-party mod versions and their available APIs.

## Expand your economic options

Install Admin Shop Prices to manage individual supported prices. Install Additional Currencies for currency display and supported historical exchange rates.

## Controls

Open economy menu: Right Ctrl + K.
