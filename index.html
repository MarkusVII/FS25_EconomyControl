Features

01

Historical trends

Choose a starting year and experience economic conditions adapted from historical data.

02

Independent markets

Crop, fuel, fertilizer, machinery and land prices can evolve differently.

03

Beyond 2026

An internal simulation continues the economy after the historical period.

04

Economic calendar

The mod's own calendar leaves physical seasons, weather and fields unchanged.

05

Currency & services

Supported exchange rates and economic operations are adapted to the chosen country.

06

Optional integrations

Use Economy Control independently or combine supported third-party systems.

  3. In-game economy menu: Right Ctrl + K. Platform: PC/Mac.
